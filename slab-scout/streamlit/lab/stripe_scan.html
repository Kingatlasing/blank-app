Stripe Scan

This screen shows shifting stripes, and the card reflects them. Your phone films the reflection, and Slab Scout reads dents, creases, scratches and scuffs from how the stripes bend. That includes flaws you can't see in a normal photo.

Set up (2 minutes)

  1. Darken the room. Turn the screen's brightness all the way up.
  2. Lay the card flat on the table, a little in front of the screen. Tilt the screen back until you can see it reflected in the card's surface.
  3. Prop the phone so it looks at the card from the other side, roughly where your eyes are when you see that reflection. It must stay completely still for the whole scan, so lean it on something.
  4. On the phone: 2× zoom, the card filling most of the picture, 4K video. Press and hold on the card until AE/AF LOCK shows.
  5. Start recording, then press Start scan here. Stop recording when the screen flashes white the second time.

The stripes should look sharp in the card's reflection, about 8 to 15 stripes across the card. If they look blurry, make them wider. If you only see a few, make them narrower.
